# RentUK privacy notice

Last updated 17 November 2026. The same notice is in the app under More → About.

## Your property data stays on your device
Properties, tenants, checks, dates and costs are saved only in your browser on your device. We never receive them. Backup files are saved wherever you choose. "Delete all my data" removes everything from the device.

## No tracking
No accounts, cookies, analytics, adverts or trackers. The browser's own storage is used only to make the app work.

## If you use the chat
Your question, the earlier questions and answers in that chat and, only if you tick the box, the property's scores and totals (no names or contact details) go through our small relay on Cloudflare to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, to get an answer.

- The relay does not log or store them.
- Cloudflare handles your internet (IP) address to deliver the message and limit misuse; we do not keep it.
- Anthropic handles your question under its own privacy policy and may keep it for a limited time.
- Please don't type names, addresses or other personal details into the chat.

## If you email us
Your message reaches us through a forwarding service (addy.io), which hides our address. We use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them if you ask.

## Questions or requests
Use "Email us" in the app under More → Feedback.
