RentUK

Your rental, calmly sorted. A free, private app for UK landlords: never miss rent, a repair or a legal check.

  • Rent tracking: see what is due, paid and overdue.
  • Legal checks and reminders: gas safety, electrical safety (EICR), energy certificate (EPC), smoke and CO alarms, deposit protection and Right to Rent.
  • Rules for England (including the Renters' Rights Act), Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
  • Repairs, documents, costs and a simple tax estimate.
  • No sign-up, no adverts, no tracking. Your property data stays on your device.
